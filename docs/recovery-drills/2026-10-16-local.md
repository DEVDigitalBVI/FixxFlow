## Findings

The generic Supabase PostgreSQL image required the drill to reconcile two managed role
definitions (`supabase_functions_admin` and `supabase_realtime_admin`) and point
`pg_cron` at the isolated target database. No passwords were copied. The final replay
retained grants and RLS rather than skipping privilege restoration.

## Limitations and required follow-up

This is not proof of the production RPO or RTO. It did not test a Supabase physical
backup or PITR recovery point, production data volume, Storage object bytes, Auth and
Realtime settings, Edge Functions, external integrations, DNS, or application
reconnection. Complete a Supabase restore-to-new-project rehearsal after management
access is available, keep processing disabled, run canonical readiness/smoke checks,
and record the measured end-to-end recovery time before certifying the four-hour RTO.
